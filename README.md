# BrandiFi Portfolio

## Put it live with GitHub Pages
1. Create a free account at github.com and click **New repository**. Name it `yourusername.github.io` (this gives you a clean URL) and set it to Public.
2. Click **uploading an existing file**, drag in everything inside this folder (index.html, style.css, script.js and the assets folder), then click **Commit changes**.
3. Go to **Settings > Pages**, set Source to **Deploy from a branch**, choose `main` and `/ (root)`, then Save.
4. After a minute your site is live at `https://yourusername.github.io`.

## Things to edit
- **Logo:** put your logo at `assets/logo.png` (transparent PNG works best). It appears in the nav, the About card and the browser tab.
- **Colors:** change `--a` and `--b` at the top of `style.css` to match your logo.
- **Contact:** in `index.html`, search for `YOUR-EMAIL`, `YOUR-NUMBER` and the `#` social links and replace them.
- **Videos:** add a new MP4 to `assets/videos`, a poster JPG to `assets/posters`, and copy one `<article class="card">` block.
